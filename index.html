What we heard

We will share grouped findings here first, so people who contributed can see what the community told us before the findings are shared more widely.

Because the survey is anonymous, individual responses will not be attributed to anyone. Results will be presented as themes, patterns and aggregated insights.

Community first

Participants will have a public place to return to and see the findings without needing to provide an email address.

Anonymous by design

We focus on what is being heard across responses, not on identifying individual people.

Useful evidence

Insights can help inform community conversations, support services, education and policy.

About GIC

Gambling Insights Collective begins with a simple idea: people and communities are experts in their own experiences. Rather than starting with assumptions, we start by listening, looking for patterns, and returning what we learn to the community.

Listen

Gather anonymous community insights in ways that are accessible and respectful.

Understand

Explore themes across gambling experiences, environments, support, barriers and change.

Share

Turn what we hear into clear reports and practical insights that communities can use.
